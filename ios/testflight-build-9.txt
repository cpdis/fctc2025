Filament Coffee Track Club: tabs, Events and the Dashboard

Private build for Colin. Update the existing app. Do not delete it: your sheet connection, saved guests and pending attendance stay on your phone. You do not need a new setup code.

New since build 8:
• Three tabs at the bottom: Runs, Events and Dashboard. Record attendance on Runs, as before.
• Events: the club runs this week, upcoming specials, members near their next 50 runs, and birthdays in the next 30 days. Milestones and Birthdays moved here from Home.
• Dashboard: runs this season against last season on the same date, km run together, On a roll, the Wall, Vs last year, Every run, the leaderboard and the run log.
• Tap a runner anywhere on the Dashboard to see their runs, km, streak, club days per weekday and all-time total.
• A streak counts club days only: Monday, Wednesday and Friday (Wednesday and Friday in 2025). A special run neither adds to it nor breaks it.
• Only an "x" counts as attendance, as on the website. A "-", an emoji or a note does not, so a few 2025 totals drop by one or two.
• Attendance you record offline counts on Events and the Dashboard at once, before it syncs.
• A reminder now opens the Runs tab at once. Scanning a setup code in Settings returns to Runs and drops a reminder that was waiting for the old sheet.
• Vs last year loads last season once each time you open the app, so online it can take a moment to appear.

Please check:
• Open each tab. On the Dashboard, open the full Wall, scroll it sideways and sort it.
• Tap a runner on the Wall or the leaderboard, and check their streak.
• Turn on Airplane Mode, record attendance, and check that the Dashboard counts it.
• Compare a few totals with fctc.fun.
