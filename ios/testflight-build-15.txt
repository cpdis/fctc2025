Filament Coffee Track Club: milestone names open runner screens, and a fair rate for late joiners

Update the existing app. Do not delete it: your sheet connection, saved guests and pending attendance stay on your phone. Nothing to set up again.

New since build 14:
• On Events, tap a name under Milestones to open that runner's screen, the same one the Dashboard opens. Back returns to Events. A name stays plain if that runner has no run in the season the Dashboard shows.
• A runner who joined part way through has a "Since first run" card under their stats: the club days they made since their first run, and the rate. In All time it counts across seasons, so someone who started last October is measured from last October.
• Both ideas came from Grant's TestFlight feedback. Thanks, Grant.
