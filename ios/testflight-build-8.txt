Update the existing app. Do not delete it: your sheet connection, saved guests and pending attendance stay on your phone. You do not need a new setup code.

New since build 6:
• Ticks fill and draw, counts roll, and cards press down when you tap them.
• The Outbox shows a spinner while attendance syncs, and the Unsynced arrows on Home turn.
• Dictation and poll imports no longer tick a member for a sound-alike name. "Great run" will not tick Grant, and Tony will not tick Toby. You choose instead. Voters for a "Can't" option are not ticked.
• A row you discard in the Outbox stays discarded.
• This Week, Season and Past Runs: the tick sits after the date, and Review sits beside search at the bottom.
• A setup link now names the sheet and asks before it replaces your connection.
• Settings shows the app version at the bottom.
