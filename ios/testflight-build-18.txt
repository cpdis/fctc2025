Filament Coffee Track Club: today's run card shows when it's recorded

Update the existing app. Do not delete it: your sheet connection, saved guests and pending attendance stay on your phone. Nothing to set up again.

New since build 17:
• The Today's Run card on Runs now changes once the run is recorded. Until then it looks as it always has: your colour and a Record button. After, it turns quiet, with a tick, "Recorded", the number of runners and the distance, and an Edit button.
• If your phone has saved today's attendance but not sent it yet, the card says "Waiting to sync" with an orange icon until it reaches the sheet.
• The card's colour is still the accent you pick in Settings. It has no other meaning.
• This morning's stuck sync is fixed on the sheet's side: a run saved without its confirmation no longer holds up everyone else's.
