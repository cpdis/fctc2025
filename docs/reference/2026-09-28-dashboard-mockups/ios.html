1 · Runs tab

Today's run and recording stay first. Milestones and birthdays move to Events. Settings stays on the gear.

2 · Events tab

"What's coming": the next club runs, specials, birthdays and milestones in one place.

3 · Dashboard tab (full scroll)

Charty-style cards. Each card opens a full screen version. The hero card flips between Runs and Km.

4 · Tap a runner

Tap a name on the Wall or leaderboard. The Mon/Wed/Fri calendar shows the season and the streak at a glance.

5 · Dashboard, dark

Espresso background, crema ink. Same palette, validated for dark.

Decided 28 Sep 2026

  • Structure approved: the tabs, cards, charts and runner screen shown here.
  • Tabs: Runs · Events · Dashboard. Settings stays behind the gear on Runs.
  • Look: the app keeps its Reminders theme: system colours, the user-selected accent (green by default) and SF Pro. The pink, crema and Anton in these frames are NOT carried over.
  • Data: computed from the season the app already caches, so it works offline and updates the moment you record a run.
  • Charts: Swift Charts. No third-party chart library.
